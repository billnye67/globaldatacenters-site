Subject: Opposition to Proposed Data Center — Palm Beach County

Dear Commissioners,

My name is Jane Smith and I reside at 4521 Arden Way, Loxahatchee, FL 33470 — within 2.3 miles of the proposed Central Palm Beach data center site.

I am writing to formally oppose the rezoning application for 640 acres of data center development adjacent to FPL's West County Energy Center. My concerns include the impact on residential property values, groundwater supply, and noise from industrial cooling systems operating 24/7.
