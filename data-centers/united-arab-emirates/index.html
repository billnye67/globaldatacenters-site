Data centers in the United Arab Emirates: 15 projects mapped

15 projects tracked · 5 proposed or being built · 5 operating · 5 with no stage on record ·
6,145 MW from the 5 active projects that report a figure

The UAE's biggest data center project is the UAE-US AI Campus in Abu Dhabi, a 10-square-mile site that G42's Khazna Data Centers plans to grow to about 5 gigawatts of power. Its first piece is Stargate UAE, a 1-gigawatt cluster built with OpenAI, Oracle, Nvidia, Cisco and SoftBank. Its first 200 megawatts were due in the third quarter of 2026; in August Khazna said two 100-megawatt buildings were enclosed with equipment going in and that it was on track to hand over capacity to customers this year.

Khazna, which controls most of the country's data center capacity, says it runs about 300 megawatts in the UAE with almost 370 more under construction or commissioning. That includes a 100-megawatt campus in Ajman, 20 halls of 5 megawatts each, and 200 megawatts for Microsoft due before the end of 2026. In Dubai, the telecom company du signed a $544.54 million deal with Microsoft in 2025 for a new large data center.

The 2026 war with Iran reached the industry here. Amazon said two of its UAE data centers were struck by drones in March, and in September it said some customer data held only in the damaged facilities could not be recovered. Power is the other pressure: Wood Mackenzie estimates UAE data centers used 3 terawatt-hours in 2025, about 2% of national demand, and will use more than 6 by 2030. Khazna's commercial chief, Greg Jasmin, said the campus depends as much on new power generation and transmission as on the buildings.
