Data centers in Macon County, North Carolina: moratorium in effect

Franklin has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Jul 9, 2026, running until Jul 9, 2027.

1 moratorium or ban in effect ·
0 projects tracked

Macon County has proposed zoning limits on data centers. It is not yet in force.

No data center project in Macon County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.
