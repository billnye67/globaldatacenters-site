Data centers in Cherokee County, North Carolina: 2 projects mapped

2 projects tracked · 2 operating ·
117 MW from the one active project that reports a figure

Core Scientific has turned its crypto mine in Marble, a former American Thread plant of about 250,000 square feet on Palmer Lane, into an AI data center with 117 megawatts of power capacity. The county has no zoning, so the change needed only permits. It is one of three crypto mines that opened in Cherokee County from 2021, and the county has no moratorium in place.

The site plan filed in 2025 included more than 170 diesel generators, according to county commissioners and records obtained by Commissioner Ben Adams. Most of its water comes from three wells on site, with some from the Marble Community Water System, and Andrews turned down a wastewater hookup. "I wouldn't wish this on any county or entity ever," said Andrews Mayor James Reid. "It's absolutely destroyed Marble." The county collected $268,000 in taxes from the site in 2024, its last full year as a crypto mine, and is having the data center equipment appraised.

Near Murphy, Rebecca and Tom Lash have lived with a crypto mine's hum since 2021. "There was nothing in this little pasture but these electric lines," Rebecca Lash said. "And it was just nice and quiet." In January 2026 Adams asked fellow commissioners to renew the county's lapsed crypto mining moratorium and add AI data centers: "we were kind of snookered into this AI." Others feared a costly legal fight with the federal government, and Adams, who is not running again, has since asked the planning board to draft limits.
