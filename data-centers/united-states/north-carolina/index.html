Data centers in North Carolina: 34 projects mapped

34 projects tracked · 16 proposed or being built · 12 operating · 6 stopped or paused ·
1,992 MW from the 12 active projects that report a figure
· 42 moratoriums and bans in effect

North Carolina's biggest data center under construction is Amazon Web Services' $10 billion campus of 20 buildings in the Energy Way Industrial Park near Hamlet, in Richmond County, the largest single investment in the state's history. The sharpest fight is in Stokes County, where a Charlotte developer, Engineered Land Solutions, wants to build Project Delta on 1,845 acres of forest and farmland near Walnut Cove. Commissioners voided the project's rezoning in April, so the developer has to start the approval process over.

Three of five commissioners approved the rezoning in January. "We've been working for 20 years to try to find a revenue stream like this," Commissioner Rick Morris said then; residents sued, and in March Morris finished last of eight candidates in the Republican primary. The board then voided its vote, saying the lawsuit "correctly pointed out flaws that we needed to address." The developer promises 250 to 500 jobs and up to $40 million a year in county taxes, a figure one opponent called "outlandish."

Read more

At least 37 towns and counties have paused new data centers, among them Charlotte, Durham, Asheville, Apex, Chatham County, and, since August 17, Yadkin and Alamance counties. Residents have also driven developers away: Energy Storage Solutions withdrew a $19.2 billion project near Tarboro in Edgecombe County in July as the county weighed a moratorium, and a proposal in southeast Raleigh ended in August after residents filled all 30 comment slots at its hearing. Elsewhere, Microsoft is building two campuses in Catawba County.

State lawmakers have passed no data center rules. The House approved Senate Bill 730, which would make large data centers pay the cost of serving them, 69-44 in June, but the Senate parked it in its Rules Committee, and lawmakers are not expected to take meaningful votes until after the November 3 election, if at all this session.
