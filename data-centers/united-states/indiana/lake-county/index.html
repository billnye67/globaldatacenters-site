Data centers in Lake County, Indiana: 7 projects mapped

7 projects tracked · 6 proposed or being built · 1 stopped or paused ·
1,350 MW from the 3 active projects that report a figure
· 1 moratorium or ban in effect

Both are true here. Merrillville has a moratorium on new data centers in force, and 6 projects are still proposed or being built. A pause usually stops new applications; projects filed before it, or outside its reach, can carry on. Each project page says where it stands.

Lake County's biggest data center projects are Amazon's campus in Hobart, near 61st Avenue and Colorado Street, and Sentinel Data Centers' Project Shirley in Eagle Creek Township between Lowell and Hebron. The Hobart City Council approved Amazon's $11 billion plan in January 2026, and Amazon has paid the city $47 million. The Lake County Council voted 6-0 to rezone Sentinel's 162-acre site on State Road 2, east of Clay Street; Sentinel still needs a special exception and site plan approval under the county's data center rules.

Amazon says the Hobart campus will bring more than 400 permanent jobs, but there is still no site plan; the mayor has said there could be up to 25 buildings. Residents led by Angelita Soriano have sued the city over the rezonings and tax break, and in February 2026 asked a Lake County judge to block a fill permit that lets Amazon dig and test the soil. A hearing was set for that month; no ruling has been published. "We want transparency before approvals happen not after," Soriano said.

Read more

Project Shirley is a $5 billion, four-building campus that would use about 500 megawatts of power, with about 250 permanent jobs. The county Plan Commission sent it to the council with no recommendation after a 4-4 tie. Hebron resident Dylan Ricard, who lives less than a mile away, says he has had trouble with water from his well in recent years.

Merrillville has paused new data center approvals from June 1, 2026, to May 31, 2027, after four developers approached the town, including Karis Critical, whose nine-building campus on 101st Avenue won zoning approval in 2024, Province Group and Wylie Capital. Council President Rick Bella said the town wants to watch Hobart's campus being built first.
