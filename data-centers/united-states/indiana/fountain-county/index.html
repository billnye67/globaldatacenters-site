Data centers in Fountain County, Indiana: moratorium in effect

Fountain County has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Nov 3, 2025, running until Nov 3, 2026.

1 moratorium or ban in effect ·
0 projects tracked

No data center project in Fountain County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.

Send a letter to the Fountain County commissioners

A pause on new data centers is already in force. Pauses run out, this one on Nov 3, 2026. A letter asking them to extend it or make it permanent is a public record they have to answer.
