Data centers in Howard County, Maryland: 1 project mapped

1 project tracked · 1 operating ·
No power figure reported for active projects

New data centers are on hold in Howard County until November 2, 2027. The County Council voted unanimously on June 1, 2026, for the S.M.A.R.T. Siting Act, which bars new data center construction and stops the county from accepting applications; it can end sooner if the council adopts new rules from an 11-member task force. Existing and already-approved data centers are not affected.

The county already has a few. The largest is on Eternal Rings Drive in Laurel, with another under way at the same site; the University of Maryland Medical System has one in Columbia, and Cogent Communications runs one on Race Road in Elkridge. Together data centers paid the county about $552,000 in the 2025 tax year.

The sponsor, Council Vice Chair Christiana Rigby, said the county's data center zoning dates to 1993 and the pause gives the council elected in November time to vote on the task force's ideas. "We're not looking at just a ban for a ban's sake," she said. The Sierra Club backed the pause, while NAIOP Maryland, a commercial real estate group, warned it "sends a negative market signal that may not be easily reversed." Two of the task force seats go to building trades unions.
