Data centers in Santa Fe County, New Mexico: moratorium in effect

Santa Fe County has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Jul 1, 2026, running until Jan 1, 2028.

1 moratorium or ban in effect ·
0 projects tracked

No data center project in Santa Fe County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.

Send a letter to the Santa Fe County commissioners

A pause on new data centers is already in force. Pauses run out, this one on Jan 1, 2028. A letter asking them to extend it or make it permanent is a public record they have to answer.
