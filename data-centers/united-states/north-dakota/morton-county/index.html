Data centers in Morton County, North Dakota: moratorium in effect

Morton County has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Sep 1, 2024, with no end date set.

0 projects tracked

No data center project in Morton County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.
