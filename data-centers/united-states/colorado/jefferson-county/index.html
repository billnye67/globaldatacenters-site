Data centers in Jefferson County, Colorado: moratorium in effect

Jefferson County has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted May 19, 2026, running until Mar 19, 2027.

1 moratorium or ban in effect ·
0 projects tracked

No data center project in Jefferson County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.

Send a letter to the Jefferson County commissioners

A pause on new data centers is already in force. Pauses run out, this one on Mar 19, 2027. A letter asking them to extend it or make it permanent is a public record they have to answer.
