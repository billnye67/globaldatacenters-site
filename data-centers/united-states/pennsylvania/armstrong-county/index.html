Data centers in Armstrong County, Pennsylvania: 1 project mapped

1 project tracked · 1 operating ·
1 MW reported

Armstrong County has one data center on record: Ark Data Centers operates a 1-megawatt facility in Freeport, according to the company's own site. No larger data center proposal is on record here.

Nearby, Gilpin Township adopted zoning rules for data centers on April 23, 2026, ahead of any application being filed there. The rules remain in effect with no end date.
