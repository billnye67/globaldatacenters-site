Data centers in Anderson County, Tennessee: moratoriums in effect

Clinton has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Jul 8, 2026, running until Jan 8, 2027.

0 projects tracked

Anderson County has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Jun 15, 2026, running until Jun 15, 2028.

Rocky Top has proposed zoning limits on data centers. It is not yet in force.

No data center project in Anderson County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.
