Data centers in Loudon County, Tennessee: moratorium in effect

Loudon County has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Jun 29, 2026, running until Dec 29, 2026.

1 moratorium or ban in effect ·
0 projects tracked

No data center project in Loudon County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.

Send a letter to the Loudon County commissioners

A pause on new data centers is already in force. Pauses run out, this one on Dec 29, 2026. A letter asking them to extend it or make it permanent is a public record they have to answer.
