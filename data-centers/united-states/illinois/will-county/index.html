Both are true here. Plainfield has a moratorium on new data centers in force, and one project is still proposed or being built. A pause usually stops new applications; projects filed before it, or outside its reach, can carry on. Each project page says where it stands.

Joliet approved Will County's biggest data center, the Joliet Technology Center, on March 19, 2026, and neighbors are in court trying to undo it. PowerHouse and Hillwood plan 24 buildings on 795 acres, a $20 billion campus designed for 1.8 gigawatts, or 1,800 megawatts of power. A first hearing on the city's and developers' motions to dismiss the lawsuit was scheduled for September 8 before Will County Judge Victoria Breslan; no ruling has been published.

The City Council vote was 8-1; Council member Suzanna Ibarra, who had called for more transparency, voted no. Hillwood says the campus would bring $2.1 billion to schools and other local governments over 30 years and 700 permanent jobs, and city staff say it will use a closed-loop cooling system that recycles water. The lawsuit, filed May 18 by Craig and Rhonda Doorneweerd and Pedro Garcia of unincorporated Elwood, says the campus would draw 100,000 to 150,000 gallons of water a day from an aquifer expected to fall short of peak demand by 2030.

Read more

The suit also argues that the public notice for the plan commission meeting never mentioned a data center and that the council vote broke the Open Meetings Act. The city's lawyer answered that voiding a vote taken at an open meeting would be "too extreme a remedy."

Near Braidwood, a data center by Western Hospitality Partners and Found Digital at Interstate 55 and Reed Road, near the Braidwood nuclear plant, is on hold; the city said in January 2026 the project was on hold because of ComEd's internal demands and regulations. Cielo Digital Infrastructure lists a 141-acre, 300-megawatt project in New Lenox, with no site made public. Plainfield has paused data center applications until October 18, 2026, and Mayor John Argoudelis said an extension is "highly likely."
