Data centers in Jefferson County, Washington: 1 project mapped

1 project tracked · 1 operating ·
No power figure reported for active projects

North Olympic Peninsula Data Centers runs a small colocation facility at 213 West Patison Street in Port Hadlock-Irondale, the only data center site tracked in Jefferson County. It rents secure cabinet space starting at $50 a month, with one IP address and 1 megabit of bandwidth included, according to the company's website. The business is also listed as a local internet provider on the Jefferson County Public Utility District's business directory.

This is a working local colocation shop, not a large campus proposal, so there is no megawatt figure to report and no public vote pending. Residents with questions can reach the company at 360-385-4686.
