Data centers in Calhoun County, Arkansas: moratorium in effect

Union County has a moratorium on new data centers in effect, adopted Jun 30, 2026, running until Jun 30, 2027.

1 moratorium or ban in effect ·
0 projects tracked

No data center project in Calhoun County is on our record yet. If you know of one, write to james@floridadatacenters.org and it will be added with its sources.
