QTS New Albany 2 data center, Pataskala, Ohio

785 Beech Rd SW, Pataskala, OH 43062

QTS·Proposed

The siteImagery: Esri, Maxar
Open on the globe ↗

QTS proposes its New Albany 2 data center campus in the Columbus, Ohio region. Its facility page lists two buildings at 785 and 675 Beech Road Southwest, both with New Albany mailing addresses. The project row places the site in Pataskala, Licking County.

QTS says it expects full-time technical jobs and hundreds of construction and trade jobs in the New Albany area. The company says it plans a cooling system that recirculates water. QTS lists this campus as in development.
