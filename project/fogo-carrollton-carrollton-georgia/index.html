FOGO Solutions project

FOGO Carrollton data center, Carrollton, Georgia

340 Tom Reeve Drive, Carrollton, GA 30117

FOGO Solutions·Operating

The siteImagery: Esri, Maxar
Open on the globe ↗

FOGO Solutions runs an operating data center at its headquarters, 340 Tom Reeve Drive in Carrollton, Carroll County, Georgia. The bunker-style facility offers colocation and cloud computing to West Georgia businesses, with backup power, cooling and around-the-clock monitoring. It is already up and running; no new permit or vote is required for it.

Carroll County has barred new data center, battery storage and solar farm permits since March 2026, after heavy opposition to a large data center proposed elsewhere in the county. Commissioners adopted a 100-day moratorium in early March 2026 and extended it on June 3 for another 100 days, through September 11. The moratorium covers only unincorporated parts of the county and does not apply to municipalities such as Carrollton.
