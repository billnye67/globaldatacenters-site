July 15, 2026 — DENIED

Palm Beach County denies Project Tango 5–1 — but the site keeps 2M+ sq ft of approvals

More than 12 hours. Over 80 public speakers. Commissioners voted 5–1 to deny the hyperscale AI data center near Arden and Saddle View Elementary — Commissioner Maria G. Marino was the only vote against denial. Mayor Sara Baxter recused after declaring her opposition, which Florida law bars in quasi-judicial zoning matters.

The denial was without prejudice, meaning the developer can revise and refile. And the property separately retains earlier approvals for more than 2 million square feet of warehouse and data center development that this vote did not touch. Project attorney Ernie Cox told WPTV that development “goes forward” and the vote “doesn’t have any effect on that.”

Denied · Without prejudice

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about data center opposition.

How many states have filed data center moratorium bills in 2026?

At least 12 — Georgia, Maine, Maryland, Michigan, Minnesota, New Hampshire, New York, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Vermont, and Virginia. Federal bills introduced by Sanders and AOC.

How much water does a data center use per day?

Up to 1 million gallons during peak cooling — roughly 3,300 households at the EPA’s 300-gallons-per-family-per-day average. AI data centers consumed 17 billion gallons nationally in 2023, projected to hit 68 billion by 2028.

Can I oppose a data center in my community?

Yes. Attend county meetings, send opposition letters, organize neighbors, engage state legislation. 833+ groups in 49 states are active. We send a personalized letter for $9.

Do data centers lower property values?

Research consistently shows proximity to data centers negatively impacts residential values. Full guide →

Was Maine the first state to pass a data center moratorium?

Maine’s legislature passed LD 307, but Governor Mills vetoed it April 24. She signed LD 713 stripping tax incentives instead. Full Maine tracker →

How is Google AI search related to more data centers?

Every AI query runs through a data center. Google’s AI Mode has 1 billion monthly users and is replacing traditional search. The more AI replaces normal queries, the more data center infrastructure Big Tech needs. Read our full breakdown →
