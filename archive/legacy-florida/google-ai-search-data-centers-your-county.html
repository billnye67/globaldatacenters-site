Google just killed normal search — and that means more data centers headed for your county

Every AI query runs through a data center. Google's 1 billion AI Mode users is why developers are filing rezoning applications in communities like yours right now.

AI Infrastructure National Published May 27, 2026  ·  floridadatacenters.org

On May 19, 2026, Google announced the biggest change to its search engine in 25 years. The classic list of blue links is being replaced by an AI-powered interface that generates custom answers, holds conversations, monitors the internet for you, and can even call businesses on your behalf.

TechCrunch ran the headline: “Google Search as you know it is over.” Google CEO Sundar Pichai called it the beginning of the “agentic era” — AI that doesn’t just answer questions but takes actions.

1 billion

Google AI Mode monthly active users as of May 2026 — and growing.

Nobody is saying this out loud

Every one of those AI queries — every conversation, every background agent monitoring the web for you — runs through a data center. Traditional search used one server call. An AI conversation uses dozens. The more AI replaces traditional search, the more data centers Big Tech needs to build. And they want to build them in your backyard.

$710B

What Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Meta collectively plan to spend on North American data centers through 2026. The power and water comes from communities like yours.

This is why your county commission is getting rezoning applications

The AI revolution isn’t abstract. It’s the reason your county planning board got an application for 800 acres of server farms. The demand signal is only accelerating — Microsoft has announced $80B in AI infrastructure, Amazon $150B, Google $75B, Meta $65B, all targeting 2025–2026 buildout.

Communities that act now — before an application is filed — have the most options. Once a developer has a rezoning approved, fighting it becomes exponentially harder.
