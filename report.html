Report a data center

Saw one that is not on the map?

A building going up, a rezoning sign on a fence, a notice in the paper, a name on a county agenda. Send whatever you have, even if it is only half a story. A person reads every report. Nothing you send goes on the map by itself.

Got it. Thank you.

Your report is in the queue. We check it against public records and local news before anything shows up on the map, so give it a few days.

If you left an email, we will only use it to ask you a follow up question about this report. You are not signed up for anything.

Tell it plainly. What is there, what is being built, or what you heard. If you know the company name or the project name, put that in too.

Please tell us a little more about what you saw.

A street address is best. Cross streets, a landmark, or just the town works fine too.

Please tell us where it is, even just the town.

Please tell us which country it is in.

A news story, a county agenda, a permit, a Facebook post. Anything that backs up what you saw.

Please paste a link that starts with http:// or https://

A photo, if you took one Optional A picture of the building, the sign, or the notice. Up to 5 MB. Only we see it.

That photo is larger than 5 MB. Please pick a smaller one.

Only if you are fine with us asking a follow up question. We will not add you to the mailing list and we will not publish it.

No account, no captcha. We keep your report and the email you give us, nothing else.

What happens after you hit send

  1. It goes in a queue

    Straight into a review list only we can see. It is not published, and it does not touch the map.

  2. We check it

    We look for the permit, the agenda item, the filing, or the news story behind it. That is what every entry on this site is built on.

  3. It goes on the map, with its sources

    If it holds up, it gets added with links to the records we found. If we cannot confirm it yet, we hold it and keep watching.
